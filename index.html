Volume I · The Dispatches Cloverdale, Sonoma County 266 Acres · Asti Road

Ground Truth

A documentary record of the 266-acre Masonite wood-treatment and former Louisiana-Pacific mill site south of Cloverdale — what was made here, what still lies in the ground, and what is now proposed to be built over it.

Compiled · Spring 2026

Eight dispatches make up this record. The first four set down what the public documents say; the last four let you see the ground itself. Start anywhere — but if you read only one, read the chronicle.

I · In the record

What the documents say

  • The Chronicle · 266 Acres South of Town The full documentary history — Masonite’s wood-treatment plant, four decades of mill work, the copper-chromium-arsenic-and-PCP contamination, two decades of groundwater monitoring, the quiet January 2026 case closure under a deed covenant, and the 605-unit Esmeralda village now proposed for the parcel. groundtruth.watch/chronicle.html
  • Telling the Ground Truth A plain-language telling for the neighbors — what the record actually means, read through an older idea: that a poisoned place you cannot leave must be kept in living memory, not merely filed in a county drawer. groundtruth.watch/telling.html
  • Sources & Documents The documentary spine. Every public record the chronicle rests on, with notes on what is hosted durably on government servers, what sits on the developer’s law-firm file-share, and what the City of Cloverdale has not posted online at all. groundtruth.watch/sources.html
  • The Public Record Every comment submitted on the 2009 Alexander Valley Resort EIR — the earlier project for this same ground — paraphrased into plain present-day language, with the City’s formal responses kept verbatim. groundtruth.watch/comments.html
II · On the ground

What the parcel shows

  • A View from Above Historical aerials of the parcel — 1961, 1971, 1983, 2005, and 2009 — set against the 2026 Esmeralda site plan. Drag to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom; switch years to watch the mill appear and vanish. groundtruth.watch/viewing.html
  • Molalla / Masonite Mill, 1965 A single annotated aerial frame of the wood-treating plant and sawmill at work, with zoomable callouts marking the tanks, ponds, and treated-wood yard — the Restricted Soil Area the 2013 land-use covenant now governs. groundtruth.watch/underfoot.html
  • Cloverdale, 2018 / 2024 A side-by-side wipe of USDA imagery six years apart — the parcel as it actually sits today, the do-nothing baseline against which the proposal is measured. One finger wipes; pinch to zoom. groundtruth.watch/status-quo.html
  • About Construction Dust An interactive wind model showing how arsenic- and dioxin-bearing dust can drift past a fully OSHA-compliant work site during Phase 1 grading, beside the capped contaminated soil. Plausibility, not measurement — no off-site air samples exist. groundtruth.watch/dust.html

About this index

This is the front page of the Unraveling project — a citizen’s record of the 266 acres south of Cloverdale. The dispatches above are compiled from the Cloverdale Reveille archive, the Regional Water Board’s GeoTracker file, the project Environmental Impact Report and its addenda, and contemporary reporting. Mistakes and hallucinations will most certainly be made.

The record will grow as the regulatory and entitlement processes unfold. If you hold a public document, comment letter, or piece of correspondence that belongs here, the compilers welcome the addition. Errors of fact, omission, or emphasis should be reported back so that later versions can correct them. Distribute freely; correct generously.
