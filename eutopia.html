    This is a made-up story to teach a real rule. Cibola, Eutopia, the old resort, and Compound X are invented. The rule is real: CEQA section 15162 — when a new fact forces a project to redo its environmental study, and when a short update is enough instead. This is not legal advice, and it is not about any real place or person.

    The rules, plainly

    One rule decides it — §15162, and it’s a tangle: it sets the high bar for forcing a new study. A second, §15164, comes into play once you’re past that bar — it governs the short update you may do instead. Whatever role you pick, these don’t change.
