The Break-Even

A fixed cost that scales with neither the number of lots nor the passage of time still moves the point where a project first turns a profit. The developer’s own model, with one line added.

Compiled August 2026

A first-order model for entitlement-driven development sets profit this way:

profit(N) = N×price_per_lot − total_cost

Total cost is land, infrastructure, and the interest carried while the infrastructure is built. Break-even is the number of lots that has to sell to clear that total; below it, the project loses money.

The sliders and their ranges below are the developer’s own. One term is added—covenant_cost, the fixed capital a recorded environmental covenant adds to a contaminated site—and it is folded into total cost, so the line below is the developer’s model carrying that cost. Red marks where the project runs at a loss; green, where it clears its costs.

as published    profit(N) = N×price_per_lot − land − infra − carry
plotted here   profit(N) = N×price_per_lot − land − infra − carry − covenant_cost

covenant_cost is the capital a recorded environmental covenant adds to the dirt-work. On the Asti Road parcel, the 2013 Covenant and Environmental Restriction (Sonoma County, Doc. 2013088084) bars any excavation within the Restricted Soil Area except in strict compliance with the approved Soil Management Plan, requires all excavated soil to be handled and disposed under that plan, and requires every remedial measure to be preserved—obligations that bind each successive owner and, by their own terms, run in perpetuity.

Method & sources

The model, its sliders, their ranges, and every default value are copied from Devon Zuegel, “Land development math is alchemy” (devonzuegel.com, April 6, 2026). Its identities: carry_cost = total_infra_cost × interest_rate × years; total_cost = land + infra + carry + covenant_cost; IRR = (revenue / total_cost)^(1/years) − 1. The red area is loss (left of break-even), the green area is profit (right).

The covenant_cost term is added here and appears in no version of that model; it is folded into total cost, so the single line plotted is the developer’s model carrying that cost. Break-even is (land + infra + carry + covenant_cost) / price_per_lot. With covenant_cost at $0 the readout reproduces the source exactly—$210M revenue, $79M cost, $131M profit, 62.4% margin, $9.0M carry, 63.0% IRR; above $0 the covenant capital lowers profit and pushes break-even right. Every value is an illustrative default, not a figure from any project pro forma—the magnitude of the added term is a slider, not an estimate, and a one-time capital figure understates a covenant cost that recurs with each ground-disturbing phase.

Covenant terms: Covenant and Environmental Restriction on Property, Alexander Valley Resort, recorded Sonoma County Sept. 13, 2013 as Doc. 2013088084, Art. III §3.1(e) and Art. IV §4.3.
